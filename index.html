Zakat Calculator Privacy Policy

Developer: Junaid Khan Niazi
Last updated: 7 October 2026

Data we collect

None. Zakat Calculator does not collect, upload or share any personal information. There is no sign-in, no ads and no analytics, and the app does not use the internet.

Data on your device

The rates and amounts you type are saved only on your phone so they are still there next time you open the app. They never leave your device.

Deleting your data

Open the app, tap the shield icon (About and privacy), then tap "Delete all data". Uninstalling the app also removes everything it saved.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and shown in the app.
